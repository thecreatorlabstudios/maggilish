Before & after your visit

A few simple ways to get ready and care for your results. Your appointment-specific instructions always come first.

Before your visit

Arrive with a clean canvas.

Skip mascara and eye makeup before a lash or brow appointment. A clean starting point leaves more time for your service.

Make it personal

Bring your inspiration.

Save a photo of the look you like and share what you want to keep about your natural features. Talk it through before your service begins.

After your visit

Keep your aftercare close.

Ask Maggie for the care instructions for your specific treatment, including when to use makeup and how to cleanse. Follow the timing you are given.

Between appointments

Be gentle with your results.

Avoid tugging or picking at your lashes and brows. If you are unsure about a product or your next appointment, ask before trying something new.

Have a question?

Bring it to your visit or send Maggie a message. She can walk you through your service and the aftercare that goes with it.
