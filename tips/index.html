Your glow. Her passion.

Before & after your visit

A few simple ways to get ready and care for your results. Your appointment-specific instructions always come first.

Before your visit

Arrive with clean lashes.

Skip mascara and eye makeup before a lash appointment. A clean starting point leaves more time for your service.

Make it personal

Bring your brow inspiration.

Save a photo of the look you like and share what you want to keep about your natural brows. Talk through the shape before your service begins.

After your visit

Keep your aftercare close.

Ask Maggie for the care instructions for your specific treatment, including when to use makeup and how to cleanse. Follow the timing you are given.

Between appointments

Give your lashes a gentle touch.

Avoid tugging or picking at your lashes. If you are unsure about a product or your next appointment, ask before trying something new.

Have a question?

Bring it to your visit. Maggie can walk you through your service and the aftercare that goes with it.
